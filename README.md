Gulf Horizon Energy | Oil & Gas Production Intelligence & Operational Performance Analytics

End-to-End Business Intelligence & Business Analysis Portfolio Project

Industry: Oil & Gas
Project Type: Independent portfolio case study
Company: Gulf Horizon Energy (fictional)
Data: Synthetic, for demonstration purposes
Tools: SQL, Excel, Power Query, Power BI, DAX, Jira, Confluence, Draw.io

Project Overview

Developed an end-to-end oil and gas production intelligence solution to analyze production efficiency, operational downtime, equipment reliability, operating costs, and field-level performance.

The project simulates a UAE-based energy company’s business intelligence requirements and demonstrates the complete analytical lifecycle, from business requirements gathering and data validation to interactive dashboard development and stakeholder reporting.

Business Problem

Oil and gas operations generate large volumes of production, equipment, and cost data. Without centralized reporting, management may struggle to:

* Monitor actual production against targets
* Identify production shortfalls
* Understand equipment downtime and failure causes
* Evaluate field and well performance
* Track operating costs and profitability
* Make timely operational decisions

Solution Delivered

Designed a five-page interactive Power BI reporting solution:

Dashboard Page	Business Purpose
Executive Overview	Monitor high-level production and operational KPIs
Production Intelligence	Analyze production variance, shortfalls, and efficiency
Downtime & Reliability	Identify equipment failure patterns and production losses
Cost & Profitability	Evaluate operating costs and financial performance
Field & Well Performance	Compare production and downtime across fields and wells

Key Performance Indicators

* Total Actual Production
* Total Target Production
* Production Variance
* Production Efficiency %
* Production Shortfall (BOE)
* Total Downtime Hours
* Total Downtime Events
* Estimated Production Loss
* Active Wells
* Field-Level Operating Performance

Technical Implementation

SQL: Data preparation, validation, and analytical querying.

Excel and Power Query: Source data preparation, transformation, and cleaning.

Power BI and DAX: Data modeling, KPI measures, interactive visualizations, slicers, and report navigation.

Jira: Agile backlog management, user stories, and delivery tracking.

Confluence: Business analysis and project documentation.

Draw.io: End-to-end business intelligence process flow diagram.

Business Analysis Deliverables

The repository includes documentation covering:

* Project Charter
* Business Requirements Document (BRD)
* Functional and System Requirements (FRD/SRS)
* Data Dictionary and Data Model
* KPI Catalog
* User Stories and UAT
* Requirements Traceability Matrix (RTM)
* Stakeholder and RACI Documentation
* Source Data Quality Assessment
* Business Process Flow Diagram

Project Workflow

Business Requirements → Synthetic Data → SQL Cleaning and Validation → Power Query and Data Modeling → DAX KPIs and Power BI Dashboards → UAT and Business Validation → Management Reporting

Repository Contents

The repository contains the Power BI .pbix dashboard, synthetic CSV and Excel datasets, business analysis documents, Jira backlog, Confluence templates, and business process flow diagrams.

Business Value

The solution demonstrates how centralized BI reporting can help operations teams identify production gaps, investigate downtime causes, benchmark field performance, and support data-driven management decisions.
